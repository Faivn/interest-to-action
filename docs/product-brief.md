
# Interest to Action: Product Brief

## The Problem

I'm someone who is constantly discovering new interests, learning opportunities, and ideas for projects I want to pursue. I capture these ideas in lists so I don't forget them, but many remain unfinished as new interests compete for my time and attention.

I've realised that collecting an idea is easy; deciding what to do with it and turning it into action is much harder. I want to explore how to make that transition easier without creating another complicated productivity system to maintain.

## The Opportunity

Existing tools make it easy to capture, organise, and track ideas. However, I want to explore an experience that goes beyond storing intentions: one that helps people clarify what they want to pursue and identify a realistic next step.

## The Product

Interest to Action is an AI-assisted product that helps users capture their interests, clarify their intentions, and turn them into actionable plans.

Rather than generating an overwhelming list of tasks, the product focuses on helping users make deliberate choices and commit to a manageable next step.

## The Role of AI

AI helps users clarify their ideas, identify obstacles to getting started, and generate a small number of practical action plans. Users remain in control of their priorities and choose or edit the plans they want to pursue.

## Initial Scope

The first version will focus on one core journey: capturing an interest, using AI to turn it into an actionable plan, and saving that plan for follow-through.
